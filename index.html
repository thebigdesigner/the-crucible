Programmes

How we train

Three rhythms, one goal: come out of the fire better than you went in.

01
Self-building · CRUCIBLE · The Minded

The Minded

Self-building comes first. We meet weekly to learn practical habits, mindset tools, and ways to stay focused in a world full of distractions. Master your mind, build discipline, manage your time, and grow confidence. The goal is simple: be solid on the inside before you try to change anything on the outside.

Weekly
02
Leadership · CRUCIBLE · The Forge

The Forge

We don’t wait till we’re older to lead. You’ll learn how to lead yourself, influence your friends, communicate with clarity, and stand for what’s right, in school, online, and everywhere. Our monthly session is where we talk about pressure, failure, and tough seasons, and learn how to stay strong, bounce back, and turn tests into testimonies.

Monthly
03
Impact · CRUCIBLE · Vessels of Honor

Vessels of Honor

Impact is the result. Growth means nothing if it doesn’t touch other people. We build real-life skills: communication, creativity, money, problem-solving, and personal brand, and learn how to take our gifts and turn them into value for our families, schools, and communities.

Real-life skills
